# NutriAI Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you register for NutriAI, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted password.

### Profile & Health Data
During onboarding you provide optional personal data including date of birth, gender, height, weight, target weight, activity level, and dietary preferences. This information is used solely to calculate personalized calorie and macro targets.

### Meal Images & Nutrition Data
When you photograph a meal, images are transmitted to our servers for AI-powered food recognition. Images are stored in private encrypted storage and are accessible only to your account.

### Usage Data
We collect anonymized analytics events (e.g., feature usage, session duration, crash reports) to improve the application.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- **Nutrition Tracking**: Provide personalized calorie, macro, and water targets.
- **AI Food Recognition**: Analyze meal photos using third-party AI models (Google Gemini) via server-side APIs. Your images are never sent directly from your device to third-party services.
- **Progress Insights**: Generate historical trends and charts.
- **Account Security**: Authenticate sessions, prevent abuse, and detect unauthorized access.

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest.
- Authentication tokens are stored in platform-native secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
- Meal images are stored in private S3-compatible object storage accessible only via short-lived signed URLs.
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a work factor of 12.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal data. We may share data only:
- With AI service providers (server-side only) for meal analysis.
- When required by law or legal process.
- With your explicit consent.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- You may delete your account at any time via the Profile screen.
- Upon deletion, all personal data, meal images, and analytics associations are permanently removed within 30 days.

## 6. Children's Privacy

NutriAI is not intended for use by individuals under 13 years of age.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance.
