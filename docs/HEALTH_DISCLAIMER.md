# NutriAI Health & Nutritional Estimates Disclaimer

**Last Updated:** October 2026

## 1. Important Medical Notice

**NutriAI is not a medical device, nor is it a licensed healthcare or nutrition provider.**

The calculations, nutritional breakdowns, calorie totals, macronutrient recommendations, weight forecasts, and artificial intelligence-based food identifications provided by NutriAI are algorithmic estimates intended strictly for general wellness and educational tracking.

## 2. Artificial Intelligence Estimates & Inaccuracies

- **Computer Vision & Food Recognition**: Meal photo analysis is powered by advanced machine learning models. Factors such as lighting, food preparation methods, hidden oils, portion size variance, sauces, and ingredient density mean AI estimates may differ significantly from laboratory nutritional values.
- **Portion Size Adjustments**: Users are strongly encouraged to inspect and edit portion sizes, ingredients, and cooking preparations before confirming logs.
- **Allergens & Dietary Restrictions**: Never rely solely on NutriAI to verify whether a food item is free of allergens (such as gluten, nuts, dairy, or shellfish) or suitable for clinical dietary constraints (such as renal, diabetic, or ketogenic medical diets).

## 3. Consult Healthcare Professionals

Always consult a certified medical practitioner, physician, or registered dietitian before:
- Initiating a calorie deficit or surplus,
- Changing macronutrient ratios drastically,
- Fasting or undertaking severe dietary modifications,
- Tracking meals to manage medical conditions (e.g., Type 1 or Type 2 Diabetes, hypertension, eating disorders).

## 4. Emergency Situations

If you experience an acute allergic reaction, dizziness, weakness, or any medical emergency, call your local emergency services (e.g., 911, 112, 999) immediately. Do not consult or rely on this mobile app in medical emergencies.
